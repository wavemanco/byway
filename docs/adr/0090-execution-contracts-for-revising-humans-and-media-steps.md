## Context

The verification grouped the review's F09–F12 (cluster F) under one root cause: the runtime did not execute what each step's declaration, prompt and product copy promise, and where they differed it had no honest degraded outcome. A shortfall was accepted silently, turned into a fatal `ContractMissed`, or turned into futile paid repairs. The wrong assumptions sat at four boundaries. [ADR-0089](0089-recipe-ir-optional-reads.md) records the first (the recipe IR could not express an optional read). This ADR records the other three.

- **A person revising a port (F09).** A person's react step that consumes and produces the same text port was treated as writing that port from blank. C7's `spec` checkpoint asked the generic VISION-lite question with an empty box, and the answer replaced the interview's numbered questions and defaults (`interpreter.ts`, `out.brief = text`). Even wired, implement would have received "3: keep the CSV header" with no question 3 and none of the defaults the person accepted by leaving them alone. The scope cut, deliberately blind (C7-DR7), reads only the change and its "Why:", so behaviour the spec asked for could be cut as unrequested, and the "Trim the scope" explainer claimed the reviewer lists "what the task did not ask for", which it cannot know.
- **The shot check's evidence (F10).** Inside each C5 relay link, `render` showed the whole storyboard's text and every shot's pre-chain ASSET still; it never saw the clip. A clean and a defective clip sent byte-identical checks, and the shot-1 and shot-2 checks were identical too. A flaw in any still drove paid regenerations that could not change the check's input (9 video calls instead of 3, with a cap question per link). With no video key the check still ran and answered "No defects." for clips never made.
- **The ASSET brief contract (F11, F12).** The runtime decided how many images a step may ask for by medium ("at least one, at most the slots knob"), while each prompt stated its own count; C3's prompt allowed an empty list that the runtime refused, and a second empty answer failed the run late. Slot identity was re-derived differently at each use (trimmed and uppercased to validate, raw to deduplicate and attach, `Number()` to announce), so `" 1 "` bought an image that never attached and `"1"` plus `" 1"` bought four for three shots. Coverage was "at least one". Per-slot failures were swallowed, and the relay anchor was whichever image was made first, so a missing or failed shot 1 silently made shot 2's still the start frame of shot 1's paid clip, against [ADR-0069](0069-product-c5-relay-and-honest-skips.md) and spec §3.2.

## Decision

### 1. A person's reaction revises its port in place (F09)

- **The shape.** A person's step whose checkpoint is a reaction (`humanKind` 'react') and that consumes and produces the same text port (`brief` or `taste`) revises that port (`revisesInPlace`, `workflow/wording.ts`). The rule is keyed on the step's shape; in the catalog only C7's `spec` has it (C4's VISION-lite consumes `intent`; C6's reaction writes `taste` from `brief`).
- **The checkpoint.** When the port has text, the checkpoint opens with it as `initialText` (the web's ReactPanel prefills it), and asks the person to revise it in place: for VISION-lite, "Answer the questions below in place, or change their defaults; anything you leave as it is keeps its default. What you send is the brief the next step works from." (`SPEC_IN_PLACE_PROMPT`); otherwise a neutral "Revise this brief (direction) in place" question. With no text it asks as before. The answer replaces the port, as before, so the questions, the answers and the defaults left alone reach the next step together.
- **The implementer.** `product.c7.implement` v3 calls the brief the spec, and asks its "Why:" to name the spec's requirements that shaped the change: the one channel the blind scope cut reads. The brief does not go to the blind passes (C7-DR7).
- **The copy.** "Your direction" (C7) says to answer the questions or change their defaults; "Trim the scope" (and the chooser's C7 help) says the reviewer sees only the change and its "why".

### 2. The ASSET brief contract (F11, F12)

- **The count is the prompt's.** Every image-briefs template declares `images:` in its front matter: `each` (one per storyboard shot or option, as the work lists them) or a range `min-max`. The template parser requires it on image-briefs templates and refuses it elsewhere. The renderer returns it as `RenderedPrompt.images` (an additive contracts field). Declarations: `each` on `product.c5.anchor-frames`, `product.c2.mood-boards` and `helper.asset.briefs` @media and @options; `1-3` on `article.ASSET` and `helper.asset.briefs@web-page`; `0-3` on `helper.asset.briefs@prose`; `0-1` on `product.c3.asset`. Tests hold `each` to the media and options templates and the "empty list" wording to a minimum of 0.
- **C3 keeps its "no image" answer (owner decision pending).** The verification recommended removing the model's veto in favour of the person's "yes" at the optional gate (review §4, decision 3). That decision is the owner's, so C3's prompt keeps its line "If the piece is better with no image at all, return an empty list", declares `0-1`, and the runtime now honours an empty answer instead of forcing a purchase or failing the run. C3's "Custom imagery" explainer says what runs today: "If you want one, the AI makes a header image for the piece, unless it judges the piece better without one." (round 2; it said the AI makes one). If the owner removes the veto, that clause goes with it. The optional step's question ("It is meant for when you want a header image… Yes runs it") and the chooser's C3 help promise no image and are unchanged.
- **One slot plan** (`runner/src/activities/slot-plan.ts`).
  - On media and options the plan lists the shots (by number, ascending; `first` is the lowest) or the option labels, read from the work before any call: a storyboard or option map that cannot be read stops the step before anything is asked or bought.
  - On prose and web pages it is the declared range, at most the step's `slots` knob.
  - One identity per slot: a shot number however written (`" 1 "`, `"Shot 1"`, `"01"`), an option label in either case (`"a"`, `"Option A"`), a trimmed name. Checking, deduplicating (the last brief for a slot is kept), buying, attaching, announcing and the anchor all use it.
  - Coverage is checked before anything is bought: on media and options a missing slot is a miss (its problem names any unknown slots too); elsewhere fewer than the minimum is ("Write at least one image brief.", as before). An answer that covers every shot or option is used without asking again, even when it also names slots the work does not have: those briefs are left out, and the note says so (round 2: a paid retry for an answer that already covered everything bought nothing).
- **A short answer degrades; only a malformed one fails.** A miss that still names usable briefs carries them (`Usable<T>` in `formats.ts`). `askModel` asks once more with the problem, as before, and hands the parser the earlier usable part: the second answer's briefs are added to the first's (a slot both name is made from the second), so no usable brief is dropped and coverage is judged on the two together. If they still fall short, `askModel` returns that usable part with the problem as its shortfall instead of throwing. A malformed second answer leaves the first's usable part as it was. When neither answer has a usable part (malformed, or cut off at the output limit), the step still fails with `ContractMissed`. (Round 1 kept only the latest usable answer, so a short answer followed by an empty one made no image and was said to have had no usable brief.)
- **What the step does with it.**
  - Zero briefs from a prompt whose minimum is 0, answered without a shortfall: no image and no integrate call, the work unchanged, finished **not skipped** with "The AI chose not to illustrate this piece, which its instructions allow, so no image was made; the work is unchanged."
  - Zero usable briefs in either answer: finished **skipped**, "The AI wrote no usable image brief, even when asked again, so no image was made; the work passes through unchanged."
  - Otherwise images are bought per slot. Shot 1's frame, which starts the relay chain, gets at least two tries (the gateway has already retried retryable errors; a refusal is tried once more). Failed slots are recorded. Every image failing still finishes skipped, as before.
  - A partial result finishes not skipped with one note. It says "Not every image was made" only when an image is missing, naming the shots or options no brief covered and the images that could not be made; briefs left out for slots the work does not have are said apart ("The brief for “4”, which the storyboard has no shot for, was left out."), so an answer that covered everything plus an extra slot is not called short.
- **The relay anchor is shot 1's frame, or none.** On media, `anchor` is exactly the image of the plan's first shot. When it was not made the note adds "No frame was made for shot N, so the video chain has no start frame." A relay link whose start frame (`linkStart ?? anchor`) is not an image is **skipped** before any model or video call: "Shot N has no frame to start from, so no clip was made." This replaces both starting shot 1's paid clip from shot 2's still and failing the run on the video provider's invalid-request. It amends, by addition, ADR-0069's relay clause and spec §3.2/§3.3.

### 3. The shot check sees its clip (F10)

- **The clip's last frame goes on its shot.** `relayLink` records `lastFrameArtifactId` beside `clipArtifactId` (a regeneration replaces both). The gateway already returned and stored that frame. `storyboardText` is unchanged (BLIND-1).
- **Render inside a link is told its link.** The interpreter passes `link: { shot, startFrame }` to `renderView` only while a relay link runs (`ports.shot` is set; `relay()` clears it on exit). The view is then that shot's line, and the frame the clip started from and the clip's last frame, each under its caption ("The frame this clip starts from:", "The last frame of the generated clip:"; `ViewRef.groups`, attached by `surfaceGroups` as RENDER-1 attaches a page's surfaces). `imageArtifactIds` stays the union, so checkpoints, critics and snapshots see the same images. Views outside a link are unchanged, so the launch gate and BLIND-1 lineups are untouched.
- **"Not checked" when there is nothing to see.** A link that made no clip (or a shot with no recorded last frame) has a view marked `unobservable` with "No clip was made for shot N, so there is nothing to check." A model check or critic inside a link whose view has nothing to judge is not asked: it finishes skipped with that reason, and a loop it reports to (`feedsGate`) ends with the new exit reason `unchecked`, treated like a clean check: no repair, no iteration event, no cap question.
- **The wording says what is shown.** `product.c5.shot-check` v2 says it sees the shot's line, the start frame and the clip's last frame; reports only defects the clip introduced that are visible in its last frame, leaving out anything already in the start frame (a regeneration from the same frame cannot remove it); and says motion between the two frames cannot be seen. The Motion explainer says each clip's last frame is checked against the frame it started from, that motion is not checked, and that without video nothing is checked. This applies [ADR-0079](0079-phase-4-runner-records.md)'s RENDER-1 ("what the critic is told it sees is what it is shown") to the shot check.

### 4. Also in this change (wave-1 follow-ups)

- **An image billed but not kept is not bought again.** This change and [ADR-0099](0099-paid-operations-keyed-to-their-caller.md) (decision 5) made it independently; merged, there is one design, ADR-0099's. `GatewayError.billed` is true when the provider was paid and byway could not use, keep or record what it bought (the gateway's `billedFailure`, code `byway-failure`, and the video path's failures after a clip was billed), false when byway knows nothing was sent, and absent when it is not known. `assetStep` stops on a billed failure instead of trying the image again, which bought it again on every try, as it stops on budget, key, accounting and `in-flight` problems; a byway failure that billed nothing (a call byway did not start) and any other refused or failed image are still tried again up to the step's tries, shot 1's frame at least twice.
  - **Stopping the step stops the run.** Every `GatewayError` fails its step for good and the run with it (ADR-0099), so a billed failure on one image ends the run. That is kept on purpose: a billed failure is a storage or ledger fault, and buying more images could repeat it. The images the step already made were billed and are kept in the artifact store, but the step stops before attaching or placing them, so they are not in the work. The step's and the run's failure says all of this: the gateway's message (what was billed, and that it is not bought again), then "That was the image for shot 2, so the run stops here, at the Custom imagery step, rather than buy it again. The image already made for shot 1 was billed too, and it is not in the work." (`billedStop` in `asset.ts`).
  - **Difference from ADR-0099.** None in the rule: which failures stop the step and which are tried again is exactly decision 5's. This ADR adds only the wording above to the failure's message, keeping the gateway's code, `billed` mark and message; ADR-0099 does not describe the step's message.
- **Readiness before spend for card lineups of other media.** A model step whose run has at least three references, one of them an image, asks the page renderer (`pages.ready()`) before its first provider call, so a run whose GREAT lineup would form cards (BLIND-1) and whose renderer cannot start stops before anything is spent. This is the activity-side option [ADR-0084](0084-renderer-launch-without-secrets.md) logged; no workflow change.
- **The card-cut note reaches the step** ([ADR-0087](0087-lineup-evidence-full-words-and-disclosed-card-budget.md)): `modelStep` appends the verdict's note, so the step says whose words a card cut and how many the critic saw.
- **The card fit converges.** `fitCard` keeps the best fit and the smallest try that did not fit, interpolates between them, halves what is left when nothing has fit yet or two tries in a row came out too tall, and aims a little under the line. Text whose opening words render wider than the estimate no longer ends in "[all N words not shown]" when thousands fit. Same six-render limit and fail-closed rules.

## Alternatives considered

- **Reword the promises to match the runtime** (option A): removes what the person pays for (the C7 interview, C5's per-clip QA), and keeps the fatal short answer and the raw slot identities.
- **The review's repairs as written** (option B): a pass-through brief executor, the brief in C7's blind passes (breaks C7-DR7), frame sampling or video input inside the billed video call (a new billed-but-fatal failure point), "empty is success on prose" keyed on the medium (contradicts the reachable prompts), and coverage as a hard parse failure (the F11 harm again).
- **A generic step-contract framework** (option D): speculative.
- **Removing C3's veto** (the verification's recommendation): the owner's decision; not taken here.

## Consequences

- **The workflow bundle changes once** (the revise-in-place prefill, the link context, the unobservable skip, the `unchecked` exit, and `toStepSpec` carrying `reads`). Under [ADR-0078](0078-runs-from-older-engine-versions-end-plainly.md), runs waiting under the older bundle end plainly with "started by an older version of byway… Start it again".
- **Stored data.** No SQLite or CAS migration. A storyboard without `lastFrameArtifactId` still parses; a link view built from a clip made before this change has nothing to check and is skipped, never falsely clean.
- **Prompts.** Bumped one version: `product.c7.implement` (v3), `product.c5.shot-check` (v2), and every image-briefs template whose front matter gained `images:` (`article.ASSET`, `product.c3.asset`, `product.c2.mood-boards`, `product.c5.anchor-frames`, the four `helper.asset.briefs` variants), and `advisor.compose` (v2, ADR-0089). Ledger provenance records the new hashes.
- **Cost.** No new provider calls per clip; fewer futile regenerations. Without a video key the advisor's estimate still counts the shot-check calls per link, a conservative overestimate (logged for the backlog).
- **C1-H (Study 1) note.** Its recipe hash is unchanged. Two behaviours change on unusual paths: a twice-empty brief answer used to fail the run and now finishes the ASSET step skipped; and a brief answer that repeats a slot name (or leaves several slots empty) now buys one image for that slot, where it used to buy one per brief. `article.ASSET`'s file hash changes (its front matter declares `images: 1-3`). Study 1 is not locked ([ADR-0061](0061-study-1-technical-choices-accepted.md): changes before Stage 0 are not deviations), and no prompt hash is pinned for C1-H; if this lands after a lock, the analysis must count these as arm deviations.
- **Parked** (for the backlog): middle-frame sampling of clips (best-effort only, counted in `videoExtras` and the estimate, if ever added); C5 anchor-frames' `slots: 3` knob, ignored on media, so every shot gets a paid still though only shot 1's starts the chain; whether an anchor-frame check belongs before the chain; `asset.integrate`'s "Place every image" miss after purchase still fails the run; C2's mood-boards trigger says "for the chosen option" while the prompt and spec say one image per option (an owner question); the advisor's shot-check estimate without video.
