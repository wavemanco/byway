# The default-budget scripts behind ADR-0109

The scripts that computed each configuration's estimate and default run budget (the web app's `suggestBudget`: a quarter above the high estimate, rounded up to 50 cents) for [ADR-0109](../../../0109-money-path-before-the-first-live-run.md), with what they printed. They were kept only in the session's scratchpad under Temp; Claude brought them in on 2026-10-21, unchanged. They import the code they priced by absolute path, from a worktree or a clone of the day, so they record what was run; they are not tests that run today. "head" in a file's name or label is the head of the branch the script ran on that day.

- [2026-10-18-b1/](2026-10-18-b1/): `budgets.ts`, B1's own figures as built (fix/fx-b1), before the check of B1; its output was not kept.
- [2026-10-19-check/](2026-10-19-check/): `rec2-est.mts`, from the check of B1's records lens (REC-2), with its runs at the branch head (`rec2-head.txt`; `rec2-head-nz.txt` with a Gemini image's text and thinking priced at nothing, as before B1) and at main (`rec2-main.txt`, 8401820).
- [2026-10-21-check/](2026-10-21-check/): `money3f-est.mts`, from the check of B1's money lens and its refuters (MONEY-3), with its runs at 8401820 (main), 6e4eb65 and afcc3fd (B1's commits), and the branch head with and without a Gemini key (`money3f-head.txt`, `money3f-headNoGem.txt`).
- [2026-10-21-fix/](2026-10-21-fix/): the script whose figures ADR-0109's Consequences give, from the fix round after the check. `budgets.mts` ran on the money fix's branch and `budgets-batch.mts`, the same script, on the batch's; each compares main at 8401820 with the branch, for every key combination that changes the estimate, and adds the high estimate and budget with every OpenAI input token planned as written. `main/` holds main's `estimate.ts`, `prices.ts` and `errors.ts` at 8401820, read with `git show` and their imports pointed at the worktree. `budgets-10fbf88.txt` and `budgets-04d5a64.txt` are its runs at those two commits, which the history rewrites of 2026-10-21 made 248e6fe and f7d7d91 on main; the two files are the same. `same.mts` counts the estimates that differ between the price rows of 481b2e5 (`prices-481b2e5.ts`, a copy of `packages/gateway/src/prices.ts` at that commit, now 00401a0) and the fix's.

Not kept: the money fix's backups of the files it mutated to prove its tests (`fix-money/mut/`), its copy of OpenRouter's model list as read on 2026-10-21 (`fix-money/or/models.json`, 788 KB; the page is openrouter.ai/api/v1/models, as ADR-0109's Evidence names it), and the other estimate probes the check's lenses and refuters wrote, in their clones and the scratchpad.
