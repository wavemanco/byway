# Logs kept as evidence

Most logs behind the decision records were left out of the repository ([the evidence index](../README.md) says what was kept). These sets were brought in unchanged, on 2026-10-19, 2026-10-21, 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23, because a record's figures or a backlog row rest on them alone. (Block 2's two mutation sets also hold a summaries.md that Claude laid out from the builders' reports, each saying so.) They are kept byte for byte: the repository's `.gitattributes` leaves their line ends as written. (Until 2026-10-21, `gate-build.log` and `32-gate-build.log` were stored without the carriage returns that end three of their progress lines. On 2026-10-21 every file in these sets had the same SHA-256 as its original under Temp.)
