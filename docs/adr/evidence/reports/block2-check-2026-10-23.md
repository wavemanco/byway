### 2. MONEY-2: A resumed clip whose wait has already passed is cancelled before its status is read; a cancel that fails throws away a finished, paid clip with words saying it did not finish

- **Severity:** should-fix; **refuters:** confirmed; **where:** `packages/gateway/src/providers/fal.ts:609`
- **What is wrong:** On resume, the clip's deadline is new Deadline(Math.max(0, clipEndsAt - Date.now())). Once the acceptance time plus the wait has passed, that is a 0 ms timer. It always fires before the first status round trip, so untilEnded returns WAITED. waitedGeneration (fal.ts:554-566) then cancels the job without looking at its status. Only fal's exact answer 400 ALREADY_COMPLETED saves the clip. Any other answer goes to 'failed': a network blip, a 5xx, a 404 for an old request, or another body shape. On 'failed', the finished clip fal billed is never read. The call fails, not retryable, with "did not finish clip job ... within 30 minutes", which is false, and is abandoned as waited. This is the normal path for a relay step's second try. relayLimitMs is 5L + W + 15 minutes from the step's start, so attempt 2 always resumes after the clip's acceptance time plus W. ADR-0114 promises for that case that the clip is "polled by its id, so no clip is bought twice".
- **Evidence:** Probe: packages/gateway/test/probe-money-resume-done.test.ts in the clone, built on clip-wait.test.ts's harness. An 'earlier' row's job req-earlier is accepted at T0 and the retry starts at T0+45 min. The status poll answers COMPLETED after 50 ms, as a real round trip would. Call order: ["status req-earlier","cancel req-earlier"]: the cancel comes before the status answer. When cancel answers {status:'completed'}, the clip is read and settled at 0.352. When cancel answers {status:'failed', detail:'no answer: fetch failed'}, the call fails with "fal.ai’s model fal-ai/kling-video/v2.5-turbo/pro/image-to-video did not finish clip job req-earlier within 30 minutes ... byway’s cancel did not go through (no answer: fetch failed). It may have billed the work." Spending records then show [["waited",0.5]], and the finished clip is never downloaded.
- **When it goes wrong:** A C5 relay link's first attempt hangs after fal finished the clip, for example in the clip download, and runs past relayLimitMs (2 h at the defaults). Temporal starts attempt 2, which takes over the row and resumes req-1 with its original acceptance time, now well past 30 minutes. byway cancels at once. If that one PUT fails, the run fails with a message saying the clip was not finished. The paid clip (about $0.35-1.68) is thrown away, and the row sits at its estimate as 'may have been billed' until the person clears it.
- **Proposed fix:** Read the job's status (a free read) before cancelling it on WAITED, at least when the deadline had already passed as the poll began (resume). Cancel only a job that is not COMPLETED. On a 'failed' cancel, read the status once more and keep a COMPLETED clip. Add a clip-wait test with a status poll that takes real time and a resumed job past its wait whose cancel fails.

### 6. WEB-2: Overlapping wait saves leave the Settings select (and its announcement) on a value other than the one saved
