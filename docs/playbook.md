# Playbook: people, roles and outside actions

What happens outside byway's repository: (1) people and roles to find, hire, contract or engage; (2) outside actions: accounts, keys in providers' consoles, money, legal and licences, IRB, venues, service and GitHub settings, publishing, live runs and the dashboards that check them, and the decisions whose answer is one of these; standing duties, which recur and never close, are here too; (3) the milestones and their dates; (4) the budget: estimates, quotes and the spending ceiling. Its work is an act in the world, recorded as a dated note. Work done in the repository (code, tests, prompts, protocol text, docs, records) is in the [backlog](backlog.md), whose opening states the line; a row that moved between the two on 2026-10-31 keeps its ID, and its Details say where it was filed before. Decisions are in the [ADR log](adr/README.md); the study design is [Study 1 protocol v1.1](study1/protocol-v1.1-draft.md).

Recorded at the user's request on 2026-10-07 ([ADR-0062](adr/0062-code-first-work-mode.md)). Dates are targets from the protocol's realistic timeline, not commitments.
