## user
Overused patterns in prose (words), each with a better alternative:

- "It's not X, it's Y" contrasts → say Y directly
- Reflexive triplets: three items because three sounds complete → the real number of items
- Puffery ("testament", "pivotal", "tapestry") → a concrete fact
- Trailing clauses such as "…, highlighting X" → cut them, or make a real causal claim
- "Serves as" and "boasts" → "is" and "has"
- Wrap-ups that restate what was just said → end on the last substantive point
- Dense em dashes → varied punctuation
