## user
Overused patterns in fiction, each with the direction of a better alternative:

- Clichés and stock imagery ("the air was thick with", "a symphony of") → one concrete detail only this story could have
- Explained emotion ("she felt a pang of") and unnecessary exposition → action or subtext
- Ornate or awkward word choice → the plain, precise word
- The tidy epiphany ending ("in that moment, she realized") → end on an image or an action
- Uniform sentence rhythm, and reflexive triplets → varied rhythm
- Characters who state the theme in dialogue → keep the theme under the surface
- Names, settings or premises that also turn up in plain, unseeded stories → ones rooted in this story's world
