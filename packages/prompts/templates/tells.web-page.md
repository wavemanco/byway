## user
Overused patterns in web page design, each with a better alternative:

- Eyebrow text: small labels above headings that state what the heading already says → remove it; almost always nothing is lost
- Generic gradient backgrounds → a background image, a pattern or a solid colour
- Excessive cards and containers, boxes inside boxes → a flatter layout, such as a grid or tiles
- Too many fonts, text styles and levels of emphasis → one or two fonts and two to four text styles, with accent colours and italics used sparingly
