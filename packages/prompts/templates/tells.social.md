## user
Overused patterns in social posts, each with a better alternative:

- A "broetry" hook: one dramatic line, then a line break → state the point
- Emoji bullets → plain prose
- "Here's what I learned:" lists → one lesson, told well
- Engagement-bait closers ("Agree?") → end on substance
