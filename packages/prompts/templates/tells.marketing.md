## user
Overused patterns in marketing copy, each with a better alternative:

- Hype verbs ("unlock", "elevate", "seamless") → a plain verb and a specific outcome
- Staccato negations ("No X. No Y.") → one sentence on what is included
- Repetition for effect → say it once
