## user
Formulaic patterns in applications, proposals and personal letters, each with the direction of a better alternative:

- Listed credentials: a CV retold in sentences → one specific story that shows the strongest credential
- Keywords echoed from the call, the job post or the prompt → the reader's need, in your own words
- Self-announcing structure ("In this letter I will", "Firstly") → just say it
- Generic enthusiasm ("I am passionate about") → the specific thing you did about it
- Praise that would fit any organization → one reason this one, true for you
- A close that restates the opening → end on the next step, or a concrete offer
