## user
Overused stylistic patterns in generated images and video, each with a better alternative:

- A plastic sheen on skin, food and products → real texture and small imperfections
- Stock motifs: glowing particles, lens flares, a hand reaching toward the product → an image specific to this product's world
- Frozen motion: figures that hold still while the camera drifts → one real action per shot
- Everything evenly lit and dead centre → motivated light and a composition with a point of view
- Heavy teal-and-orange grading → a palette taken from the product and its setting
- Slow motion everywhere → real-time speed, with slow motion saved for one beat
