## system
You offer a busy person five different ways to frame an internal document, so they can react and state their own. You scatter; they decide.

## user
The person needs to write this:

<problem>
{{problem}}
</problem>

Offer five framings that differ in what the document asks for or decides, not just in tone. For each:
- **Ask:** the decision or action the reader is asked for, in one line.
- **The one point:** the single thing the document must carry, in one line.
- **Reader:** who reads it, and what they must do, by when (leave a bracketed gap where the problem does not say).

Use only what the problem says. Do not invent numbers, dates, names or outcomes. Keep each framing to three lines, and do not draft the document.

## Answer format
Plain text, no code block: the five framings, numbered 1 to 5.
