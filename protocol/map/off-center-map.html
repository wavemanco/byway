Off-Center

A protocol for distinctive AI work. It generalizes Anshu Chimala’s “How to turn your AI into a world-class designer” (Lenny’s Newsletter, Sep 1 2026) beyond design. The article’s eight techniques become lego blocks, a small grammar says how they connect, and seven configurations, plus a fiction variant (C6a), wire them for real use cases. C1 is the article’s own design workflow. Built from two research workflows, then checked by two review rounds.

01 · The idea

The default sits in the middle of the curve

Preference-tuned models settle every open decision with the most typical, please-everyone option, and they add far more readily than they remove. The article’s target is the feeling the work evokes. Distinctiveness is how you get there.

SEED · VISION

Bring variety from a seed or a human. A random string the model has to interpret, or your own taste. Never just “be creative.”

CRITIC

Separate judgment from production. A stronger critic in a fresh context sees only the rendered result, and the stop threshold never appears in its prompt.

ASSET · RELAY

Delegate to specialists. Route what the generalist agent can only fake to a specialist model, then verify it in the real render.

CUT · TELLS · VOICE

Subtract, audit and re-voice after generation, never ban before it. A human reads and rephrases every line.

03 · The grammar

How the legos snap together

Built from known agent-workflow patterns and the Double Diamond. Each operator is grounded in something the article does, except where marked ⟨A⟩. Loop failure is defined once: probe 2 iterations, cap 5 in total, stop on a plateau, return the best snapshot to a human, and backtrack only by human decision.

      06 · Use-case research

      Where the pain is, scored

      Seven research sweeps, one synthesis, and two verification rounds. Round 2 re-checked 55 claims: 37 were accurate, 17 had minor inaccuracies and 1 was wrong, and all are now corrected. Scores are one rater’s judgment against defined anchors. Evid-fix (evidence that the blocks’ mechanism actually helps this use case) is low almost everywhere.           08 · More to explore

          Off-path, but promising

          These came up in research but are substantially different from the article’s techniques, so they stay out of v1. Below is the shortlist most likely to earn a block, plus the platform channel candidate. About 70 more are catalogued in 05-more-to-explore.md.

          No correctness guarantee

          CRITIC judges taste, not truth. Facts, citations, code correctness and security need verification outside these blocks. A VERIFY block is the top candidate for v2.

          Critic reliability unmeasured

          Evidence from older models puts unanchored judges near chance on UI and prose. That is why the protocol adds a calibration step and prefers ranking against references.

          Never for evasion

          No gate or success metric uses an AI-detector score. Disclosures, platform AI labels and provenance metadata are always preserved, and nothing invents facts about a person.

          Unproven end to end

          No study yet tests any configuration as a whole, or shows a distinctive AI page converting better than a generic one. 05-more-to-explore.md proposes the experiments.
